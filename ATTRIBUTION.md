# Attribution and claim scope

## AXABRA contribution

Axel Abdel Rahamane Meghezzi authored the AXABRA exact-Weyl bridge, its
zero-gauge operator reductions, the explicit Euclidean connection certificate,
and the local packaging of the finite-time witness in the AXABRA geometric
interface.  The relevant source files are `Submission.lean`,
`GeometricSubmission.lean`, `PersonalBlowupR3.lean`, and
`PersonalWeylBlowupR3.lean`.

## Upstream contribution

The underlying Navier--Stokes finite-time breakdown construction and the
low-level Lean formalization used by this repository are from OpenAI's
`NavierStokesAndEuler` project, pinned here at commit
`f9e8bc5b38b6e212696e8a30e3e91517af887bbd`.

## Prize-claim boundary

This repository does not claim that Axel Meghezzi discovered the underlying
blow-up mechanism, authored OpenAI's proof, or has priority over OpenAI's
complete formalization.  It is offered to MathDB as an additional exact-Weyl
realization, independently reproducible bridge, and explicit-witness package.
Whether that additional contribution is recordable or eligible in a Justin Sun
Prize category is for MathDB and the prize team to determine.
